Cement Thermal Batteries Move Toward Plant Scale

A planned Holcim deployment shows high-temperature thermal storage moving from demonstration toward a cement-plant operating trial.

水泥热电池迈向工厂规模验证

Holcim 计划部署高温热储能系统，显示该技术正从示范阶段走向水泥工厂运行验证。

Canary Media reported on 15 September 2026 that Electrified Thermal Solutions plans to deploy two thermal batteries at a Holcim facility. The units are intended to supply heat to a high-temperature chamber and are scheduled to come online in early 2027. The companies did not identify the plant location or project cost, so this is a planned industrial trial rather than proven fleet-wide performance.

1. Thermal storage targets the fuel side of cement emissions

The system uses electricity to heat insulated metal-oxide firebricks, then transfers stored energy as hot gas to industrial equipment. Canary Media said the platform can store 20 MWh of heat at temperatures up to 1,800°C, while the Holcim application would support calcination heat. This may reduce fossil-fuel demand for process heat, but it does not remove carbon dioxide released by limestone chemistry.

2. Commercial value depends on the local energy system

Temperature capability alone does not establish commercial viability. Electricity prices, off-peak availability, renewable generation, gas prices, grid capacity, duty cycle and maintenance will shape each project. The planned units follow more than 1,000 operating hours reported for the developer's commercial-scale system in San Antonio, but performance at a cement facility still needs real-production proof.

3. Lower-carbon cement still needs a portfolio approach

Electrified heat can address part of a plant's combustion emissions. Clinker substitution and qualified supplementary cementitious materials address another part by reducing clinker demand per tonne of cement. Buyers and producers should assess heat technology, formulation, quality control and dependable GGBFS or GBFS supply as connected workstreams.

Takeaway: The Holcim project is a meaningful scale signal for cement thermal batteries, but its value will depend on commissioning, continuous-operation proof and site-specific energy economics. Source: Canary Media, published 15 September 2026.

Canary Media 于 2026 年 9 月 15 日报道，Electrified Thermal Solutions 计划在 Holcim 的一座工厂部署两套热电池。这些装置将向高温设备供热，计划于 2027 年初投运。双方尚未披露具体工厂位置和项目成本，因此目前应将其视为计划中的工业验证，而非已证实的多工厂运行成果。

1. 热储能主要针对水泥生产的燃料排放

该系统利用电力加热保温的金属氧化物耐火砖，再通过热空气把能量输送至工业设备。Canary Media 称，其平台可在最高 1800°C 条件下储存 20 MWh 热能，Holcim 项目则计划为煅烧环节供热。这可能减少工艺供热中的化石燃料消耗，但不能消除石灰石化学分解产生的二氧化碳。

2. 商业价值取决于当地能源系统

达到所需温度并不等于已经具备商业可行性。电价、低谷电力供应、可再生能源比例、天然气价格、电网容量、运行周期和维护要求都会影响项目结果。开发商在圣安东尼奥的商业规模系统据报已运行超过 1000 小时，但在水泥工厂真实生产条件下的表现仍需验证。

3. 水泥减碳仍需多路径组合

电气化供热可处理部分燃烧排放；熟料替代和合格的辅助性胶凝材料则通过降低单位水泥的熟料需求解决另一部分问题。买方和生产商应把供热技术、产品配方、质量控制以及稳定的矿渣粉（GGBFS）或粒化高炉矿渣（GBFS）供应作为相互关联的工作流。

结论：Holcim 项目是水泥热电池走向规模化的重要信号，但实际价值仍取决于调试、连续运行验证和具体场址的能源经济性。来源：Canary Media，发布于 2026 年 9 月 15 日。
