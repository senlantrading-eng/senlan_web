Noida Airport LC3 Use Tests Low-Carbon Cement at Scale

A newly filed project disclosure says LC3 was used for pavement-quality concrete at Noida International Airport, moving the low-carbon cement discussion from laboratory potential toward application-specific qualification.

诺伊达机场采用 LC3，低碳水泥迈向工程验证

一份最新提交的项目披露称，诺伊达国际机场的道面混凝土采用了 LC3，使低碳水泥议题从实验室潜力进一步走向具体应用场景的工程验证。

Tata Projects' Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, filed on the National Stock Exchange of India on 4 September 2026, says the company adopted limestone calcined clay cement, or LC3, for pavement-quality concrete at Noida International Airport during FY2026. The report states that the material is standardized under IS 18189:2023 and replaces part of Portland clinker with calcined clay and limestone. This is a company disclosure about one project, not evidence that every mix, site or supply chain will produce the same result.

1. A project reference strengthens the qualification signal

Infrastructure pavements place demanding requirements on concrete performance and execution. A disclosed LC3 application in this setting is therefore commercially relevant, but the key lesson is qualification rather than automatic substitution. Cement producers, contractors and asset owners still need mix design, raw-material characterization, trial data, quality control and project-specific acceptance before changing clinker factors.

2. Standards reduce uncertainty but do not remove supply risk

The report's reference to IS 18189:2023 gives the disclosed application a standards context. Yet a standard alone cannot ensure that qualified inputs are available at the required volume and timing. Calcined clay, limestone and established supplementary cementitious materials such as GBFS and GGBFS have different processing, testing, storage and transport needs. Procurement must match technical approval with dependable supply.

3. Scale-up becomes a materials-and-logistics task

As lower-clinker formulations move into larger projects, teams need clear specifications, representative sampling, compatible storage and delivery windows aligned with construction schedules. Diversification across SCM families can improve options, but materials are not interchangeable without testing. Takeaway: The Noida disclosure adds a practical reference for LC3, but responsible scale-up still rests on standards, project testing and reliable input logistics. Source: Tata Projects, Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, filed on NSE India on 4 September 2026.

Tata Projects 于 2026 年 9 月 4 日向印度国家证券交易所提交的《2025-26 综合年度报告》称，公司在 2026 财年为诺伊达国际机场的道面混凝土采用了石灰石煅烧黏土水泥，即 LC3。报告称，该材料依据 IS 18189:2023 标准，以煅烧黏土和石灰石替代部分波特兰水泥熟料。这是企业针对单一项目的披露，并不意味着所有配合比、工地或供应链都能取得相同结果。

1. 工程案例强化了认证信号

机场道面对混凝土性能和施工执行有较高要求，因此这一 LC3 应用披露具有商业参考意义，但核心启示是“先验证”，而不是自动替代。水泥企业、承包商和业主在调整熟料比例前，仍需完成配合比设计、原料表征、试验数据、质量控制和项目专项验收。

2. 标准降低不确定性，但不会消除供应风险

报告提及 IS 18189:2023，为该应用提供了标准背景。但标准本身不能保证合格原料按所需数量和时间到位。煅烧黏土、石灰石，以及 GBFS、GGBFS 等成熟补充性胶凝材料，在加工、检测、仓储和运输方面各有要求。采购必须把技术批准与可靠供应结合起来。

3. 规模化将成为材料与物流的协同任务

随着低熟料配方进入更大型项目，团队需要明确技术规格、代表性取样、适配仓储，以及与施工进度一致的交付窗口。多元化 SCM 组合可以增加选择，但未经测试的材料不能直接互换。SENLAN 在唐山经营 GBFS、GGBFS、熟料和石灰石，并依托曹妃甸支持散装材料出口协调；具体项目适用性与最终规格仍须由买方测试并协商确认。

结论：诺伊达项目披露为 LC3 增添了工程应用参考，但稳健规模化仍取决于标准、项目测试与可靠的原料物流。来源：Tata Projects《2025-26 综合年度报告》，2026 年 9 月 4 日提交至印度国家证券交易所。
