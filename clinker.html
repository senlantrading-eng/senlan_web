水泥熟料的用途及特性

纯物料介绍，不含矿粉，不掺辅料。

一、物理特性

  • 外观：黑褐色不规则块状、粒状，烧结致密，颗粒坚硬。
  • 生产：石灰石、黏土、铁质原料按配比高温煅烧至部分熔融冷却制成。
  • 不能直接加水凝固使用，必须粉碎后搭配石膏才可做成水泥。

二、化学成分与矿物特性

四种主要熟料矿物：

  • 硅酸三钙 C₃S：占 55%～65%，水化速度快，早期强度高，是水泥早期强度核心来源。
  • 硅酸二钙 C₂S：占 15%～25%，水化缓慢，早期强度低，中后期强度持续稳步提升。
  • 铝酸三钙 C₃A：占 6%～12%，水化速度最快，水化放热最高，不加石膏极易瞬间凝结。
  • 铁铝酸四钙 C₄AF：占 8%～15%，强度稳定，抗冲刷、抗腐蚀性能优良。

关键控制指标：游离氧化钙 ＜ 1.5%，保证成品安定性合格。

三、水化性能

  • 遇水发生水化反应，逐步硬化并产生强度。
  • 水化全程释放水化热。
  • 矿物配比决定凝结快慢及强度增长规律。

四、实际应用

  • 配制纯硅酸盐水泥：熟料 + 3%～5% 石膏粉磨，产出 P·I 52.5、62.5 高标号水泥。
  • 高强混凝土用料：预应力箱梁、桥梁构件、高强预制板、超高标号工业混凝土。
  • 抢修工程原料：道路修补、设备基础锚固、紧急堵漏工程。
  • 特种建材原料：灌浆料、锚固砂浆、耐磨地坪、工业特种胶凝材料。
  • 散装外贸原料：外销粉磨企业，由采购方自行加工成品水泥。

五、产品优势

  • 早强突出
  • 强度富余度高
  • 粘结性能优异
  • 适配低温施工
  • 适用高标准特种工程

Purposes and Characteristics of Cement Clinker

Pure material introduction, with no slag and no auxiliary materials added.

1. Physical Properties

  • Appearance: dark brown irregular lumps and granules with dense sintered structure and high hardness.
  • Production: made from proportioned limestone, clay, and iron-bearing raw materials through high-temperature calcination to a partially molten state, followed by cooling.
  • The clinker cannot set and harden directly with water. It must be finely ground and matched with gypsum to produce qualified cement.

2. Chemical Composition and Mineral Characteristics

Cement clinker consists of four major mineral components:

  • Tricalcium Silicate (C₃S): accounting for 55%–65%. It features fast hydration and high early strength, serving as the core source of cement’s early strength.
  • Dicalcium Silicate (C₂S): accounting for 15%–25%. It hydrates slowly with low early strength and delivers continuous, stable strength growth in the middle and later stages.
  • Tricalcium Aluminate (C₃A): accounting for 6%–12%. It has the fastest hydration speed and the highest hydration heat. Flash setting can easily occur without gypsum.
  • Tetracalcium Aluminoferrite (C₄AF): accounting for 8%–15%. It maintains stable strength and offers excellent anti-scouring and anti-corrosion performance.

Key control index: free calcium oxide (f-CaO) ＜ 1.5% to ensure qualified soundness of finished products.

3. Hydration Performance

  • Cement clinker undergoes hydration reaction when contacting water, hardens gradually, and develops structural strength.
  • Hydration heat is released throughout the reaction process.
  • The mineral proportion directly determines setting speed and the strength growth pattern.

4. Practical Applications

  • Production of pure Portland cement: mixed with 3%–5% gypsum and ground into powder to produce P·I 52.5 and 62.5 high-grade cement.
  • Raw material for high-strength concrete: widely used in prestressed box girders, bridge components, high-strength precast slabs, and ultra-high-grade industrial concrete projects.
  • Raw material for emergency repair works: applicable to road repair, equipment foundation anchoring, and emergency leakage plugging projects.
  • Raw material for special building materials: used for grouting materials, anchoring mortar, wear-resistant flooring, and industrial special cementitious materials.
  • Bulk export raw material: supplied to overseas cement grinding plants for purchasers’ independent processing into finished cement products.

5. Product Advantages

  • Outstanding early strength
  • High strength margin
  • Excellent bonding performance
  • Good adaptability to low-temperature construction
  • Suitable for high-standard special engineering projects
