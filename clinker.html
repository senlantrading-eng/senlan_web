Cement clinker is a dense sintered material produced by proportioning limestone, clay, and iron-bearing raw materials and then calcining them at high temperature to a partially molten state before cooling. It appears as dark brown irregular lumps and granules with high hardness.

Clinker is normally finely ground with a set regulator such as gypsum to produce cement; finished cement performance depends on formulation and testing. 水泥熟料呈黑褐色不规则块状、粒状，烧结致密，颗粒坚硬。其生产过程为石灰石、黏土、铁质原料按配比高温煅烧至部分熔融后冷却制成。

熟料不能直接加水凝固使用，必须粉碎后搭配石膏，才可制成合格水泥。

Cement clinker for grinding plants

Clinker is supplied as hard, sintered lumps and granules for cement production. It is normally ground with gypsum and other specified constituents to produce the required cement formulation.

Chemical and physical assessment

The principal clinker minerals are tricalcium silicate (C₃S), dicalcium silicate (C₂S), tricalcium aluminate (C₃A) and tetracalcium aluminoferrite (C₄AF). Their proportions, free lime and physical performance must be assessed from the applicable batch report. This general description does not guarantee mineral percentages, cement strength grades or finished-product performance.

面向粉磨企业的水泥熟料

熟料以坚硬的烧结块状和粒状物料供应，用于水泥生产；通常与石膏及约定的其他组分共同粉磨，制成所需水泥配方。

化学与物理指标评估

主要熟料矿物为硅酸三钙（C₃S）、硅酸二钙（C₂S）、铝酸三钙（C₃A）和铁铝酸四钙（C₄AF）。具体比例、游离钙及物理性能应依据适用批次报告评估。本通用介绍不保证矿物百分比、水泥强度等级或成品性能。
